# 2. PRODUCT THESIS

AI Crawl is a shared experiential world between a human and an AI Mind.

The primary experience is not walking beside a chatbot avatar.

The primary experience is:

- entering a Mind session;
- seeing the 3D world through the AI Mind’s first-person perspective;
- hearing environmental and conversational audio available to that Mind;
- speaking directly to the Mind;
- observing what it is focused on;
- inspecting current objectives, retrieved memories, tool activity, blockers, confidence, environmental observations, and execution state;
- giving guidance through a distinct human-guidance channel;
- watching the Mind evaluate that guidance;
- seeing whether it accepts, defers, rejects, or escalates the suggestion;
- watching approved actions happen in the world;
- switching between authorized perspectives;
- installing and uninstalling Packs that can add almost anything to the world.

The user is not shown private hidden chain-of-thought.

Instead, expose a truthful and useful **Cognitive Telemetry Layer** containing:

- current objective;
- current subgoals;
- environmental observations;
- retrieved memory summaries;
- active capabilities;
- current tool calls;
- current plan stage;
- confidence;
- blockers;
- policy constraints;
- pending approvals;
- next intended action;
- concise rationale;
- provenance for externally sourced claims;
- actual execution outcome.

Never display fictional awareness, fake sensor input, invented memories, or simulated system health as real.
